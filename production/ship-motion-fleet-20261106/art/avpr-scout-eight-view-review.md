# AVP:R Scout — independent eight-view review

## Outcome

Both V14 masters and all eight selected PNGs were opened individually. The selected generation manifest remains byte-identical (SHA-256 `fce474a68eaffe78fcb6511aa781f6851a52e2f6a0d91a0c443b679e8506fd12`). All eight selections contain three visible aft reactor mouths, with a distinct warm nose feature at right. The two corrected selections are attempt2 of forward-quarter and bank-dorsal-shallow; their initial four-reactor attempts remain rejected in the original manifest. No image, selection or repository file was changed.

No clipped hull or blocking identity change was observed. The dorsal and ventral shallow/deep progressions are clear; no permutation is requested. The deeper front exposes more of the nose assembly and makes the rear ellipses more edge-on. The deeper rear emphasizes the aft cluster but also shows more dorsal structure, so its label must not be treated as a calibrated pure yaw orbit.

## Identity and limits

The elongated dark organic hull, large swept arches, nested ribs/pipes, cyan seam lights and three warm rear mouths are retained. Individual panels, rib positions, cyan spacing and underside detail vary. The generated metal has somewhat brighter, sharper etched highlights than the profile master. These are candidates for native transition qualification, not proof of a rigid 3D reconstruction or canonical 1:1.

## Alpha and integrity

Every selected candidate is an exact byte copy of its selected generated original and selected attempt file, at 1536×1024 RGBA. Alpha is0..254; all alpha>16 silhouettes remain inside the canvas. Maximum border alpha is 1 and maximum alpha among the48 declared outside samples is 0. Hidden RGB and low-alpha residues are recorded without cleanup, normalization, resizing or other pixel edits.
