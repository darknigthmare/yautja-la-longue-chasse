Scene identity: lmb-stage-03, Ravin de cendre. An uninhabited alien volcanic canyon crossed by an ancient Yautja hunting trail. Massive cracked dark basalt cliffs with columnar vertical formations frame a deep background ravine, thin diffuse red heat-glow visible in a few distant fissures, drifting ash in a dull sulphur-orange sky. Sparse low grey blade-leaf alien plants grow only behind the combat lane near rock edges. Two small weathered dark metal hunting beacons are bolted to rocks at the far rear edges, amber lights barely visible. No structures large enough to make this an urban scene.
Composition: landscape 1536x1024, fixed side-on fighting-game camera. A wide level basalt terrace spans the entire lower 25 percent of the image as one continuous uninterrupted horizontal ground plane, straight readable front lip with natural worn texture. No gaps, steps, ramps or obstacles on this active lane; the ravine is beyond its rear edge. Layer the tall cliffs and atmospheric canyon depth behind the terrace. Keep the central midground low contrast and open so a fighter would read clearly. No receding road or diagonal playable floor.
Art direction: detailed realistic painted 2D environment, mineral textures, volcanic ash, muted charcoal/ochre palette, restrained ember-red accents in background only, distant atmospheric haze. Functional sparse alien technology consistent with a Yautja hunting outpost, original place rather than a specific movie location. High detail with coherent scale and calm environmental lighting.
